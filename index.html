What the app covers

Document tools, private storage, and backup in one app.

Scan pages, organize files, protect private documents, and restore backups when needed without jumping between separate apps.

Capture and sort Go from quick scan to a structured library fast.

Lihenium: Scan PDF & Vault keeps scanned pages, imported PDFs, folders, and recent activity readable instead of burying everything inside one endless list.

Protect sensitive files Vault features sit inside the app instead of feeling bolted on.
